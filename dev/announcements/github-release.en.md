## Stups 1.15.0 – now in English and German, in your own colour

Stups is a Kanban board, agenda and calendar for the household as a Home Assistant app.

### New

- **English and German.** The app follows your device language; *Settings › Language* switches it for you on all your devices. Push messages, nudges and error messages come in your language too. German devices keep seeing German.
- **Quick entry in English:** `Take out trash tomorrow 7pm @Ben #Home every week !!`
- **Your own colour.** *Settings › Colour* has a colour wheel; buttons, the active tab and coloured text follow at once, per person and on all devices.
- **The plus button** always creates an open card (a planned one when you pick a later date), whichever tab you are on; on the desktop there is just the one orange button.
- **Settings gear** at the top right of every tab.
- **Live sync is lighter:** when someone changes a card, other devices fetch only that card instead of the whole board.
- Add-on options, documentation and screenshots in English, plus a short demo GIF in the README.

### Earlier in this release line (1.10 – 1.13)

Compact cards, a better agenda, quick entry, and a lighter live sync.

### Install or update

Add `https://github.com/ArrestedOps/ha-stups` in Home Assistant (*Settings → Apps → App Store → ⋮ → Repositories*) or use the button in the [README](https://github.com/ArrestedOps/ha-stups#readme). Update, do not uninstall: uninstalling deletes the app's data.
